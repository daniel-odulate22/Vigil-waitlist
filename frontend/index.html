The Problem

prescription written

In Nigeria, missed doses are not inconveniences. Power cuts, zero mobile data, counterfeit drugs, and complex schedules create a cascade that ends in hospital beds and preventable deaths. Vigil exists to stop that cascade.

How It Works

Simple for patients.
Powerful under the hood.

Vigil works without internet. Without power. Without excuses.

01

Scan your medicine

Point your phone at the packaging. Vigil reads the NAFDAC number and verifies authenticity on-device. No internet required.

Offline OCR
02

Reminders fire reliably

Adaptive alerts that work when NEPA takes the light. Local triggers. Zero cloud dependency. Your schedule never sleeps.

03

Confirm or Vigil escalates

Tap to confirm. If you do not, Vigil texts your caregiver, calls you, then alerts your care team. Silence is never acceptable.

Family members get real-time updates. No more morning check-in calls. The app reports so they can stop worrying.
